# What is a Form and when it is counted as a separate storageable/boxable Pokemon

Forms are only split into separate lines when they are obtainable through normal gameplay in a state that can sit in a storage box.

Alolan Forms: In the Alola region, several Kanto-native species are only found in their Alolan Form — this is the default over the non-Alolan version, so only the Alolan Form is listed (e.g. "Vulpix (Alolan)"; the regular Vulpix is not included). This applies to: Rattata, Raticate, Raichu, Meowth, Persian, Grimer, Muk, Diglett, Dugtrio, Geodude, Graveler, Golem, Sandshrew, Sandslash, Vulpix, Ninetales, Marowak, and Exeggutor.

Oricorio has 4 Styles (Baile, Pom-Pom, Pa'u, Sensu), all of which are obtainable and boxable depending on the flower nectar fed to Cutiefly — all 4 are listed.

Lycanroc has a Midday Form and a Midnight Form, both obtainable in Sun and Moon (Dusk Form is exclusive to Ultra Sun/Ultra Moon) — both are listed.

Wishiwashi's School Form is battle-only and reverts outside of battle, so only its base (Solo) form is listed.

Type: Null has only 1 obtainable form, so it is listed once.

Minior has 7 Core Forms, one for each of its 7 unique colors (Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Indigo, Violet) — all 7 are listed. Its Meteor Form (with the hard shell) is a battle-only form and is excluded, as is its 8th, black-shiny-exclusive color.

Shellos and Gastrodon each have East Sea and West Sea forms — listed as (East) and (West).

Mega Evolution is a form, but is only available in battle and is not boxable, so it is not added to the list. This affects: Alakazam, Slowbro, Gengar, Sableye (n/a — not on list), Salamence, Metagross, Pinsir, Lucario.

Castform's weather forms are battle-only and revert outside of battle, so only its base form is listed. Mimikyu's Busted Form is likewise battle-only and excluded, leaving only its Disguised Form.
